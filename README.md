The Self-Preservation Seven (SP7) is a subtype driven by a desire for security and survival. They are practical, opportunistic, and clever individuals who create a surrogate family or support network to maintain hope and deny the inherent dangers of the world. Their focus on alliances and partnerships is key, and they are self-interested, pragmatic, and calculating. They are proactive and strategic, actively seeking opportunities for personal gain, and are shrewd negotiators. They may appear socially adept but have a deeper level of isolation, which is not necessarily physical but more about psychological distancing. They view close relationships as investments, aligning with their focus on security. However, challenges arise when their desire for pleasure and security leads them to confuse desires with actual needs, leading to panic and manipulation. The SP7 is the most astute and strategic among the Sevens, contrasting with the more idealistic and ethereal nature of the SX7.
